This audit covers every substantive homepage content section, not a pixel-for-pixel copy of its layout. The full-bleed hero, round service rail, merchandise collection, and existing interior pages remain in the design.

## Design and integration boundaries

- Typography for restored sections uses readable line heights, generous section spacing, and responsive layouts.
- Gallery photos are reused from the original site. Captions describe the images without identifying the people as actual Carroll’s staff or customers.
- The original Pay Invoice link has no working portal destination. The local invoice page provides contact assistance instead of inventing a payment service.
- Appointment, contact, careers, newsletter, and checkout remain local design previews. No information is sent, subscription created, payment collected, or financing application submitted.
- Sample merchandise remains part of the design, separate from the original repair-business content.
- Preview only at `http://localhost:3000`; no hosting or deployment performed.
